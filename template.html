General Safety Rules

1. Responsible OTC Use

Over-the-counter (OTC) medicines treat symptoms, not underlying diseases. If symptoms persist beyond a few days, stop self-medicating and seek medical advice.

2. Antibiotics & Steroids

Never self-prescribe antibiotics or steroid creams. Improper use leads to antibiotic resistance and severe skin thinning. Only take these under direct medical supervision and always complete the full course as prescribed.

3. Storage & Expiry

  • Store all medicines in a cool, dry place away from direct sunlight.
  • Keep out of reach of children and pets.
  • Never consume expired medicines. Check dates every 6 months and discard safely.

4. Hydration

When battling fevers, diarrhoea, or vomiting, maintaining hydration is often more critical than the medication itself. Use ORS (Oral Rehydration Salts) rather than plain water to replenish electrolytes.

5. When Self-Medication is Inappropriate

Proceed immediately to a clinic or ER if you experience: chest pain, sudden severe headache, difficulty breathing, unexplainable rash, sustained high fever (>103°F/39.4°C), or confusion.
